# Every modification Tinker can make

Ordered from the modification that fits the most cards to the least. Counts are from the game data (v0.113.1, 717 cards, 316 relics) and the mod's tables, generated on 2026-11-21. They are upper bounds: in a run, a value that is already at its limit, or a card the mod skips (for example Armaments once upgraded), is not offered.

**676 of the 717 cards** have at least one modification. In total there are **289 different value modifications** (cost included), and 221 of them exist on only one card.

## How this list was made

Card values: the *Upgradable* column of `tools/coverage/cards.csv` (run `tools/coverage.ps1`), plus the special-effect tables in `src/Common/Upgrades` (`ExpandedEffects`, `BidirectionalEffects`, `FixedEffects`). Trait counts use the rules in `Exceptional.cs`, counted roughly from the game data. Relics: the *Supported* column of `tools/coverage/relics.csv`.
